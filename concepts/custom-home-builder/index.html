Before you build

Good questions,
straight answers.

Still curious? Call the office and you will talk to a builder, not a call center.

What does a custom home with Ashgrove typically cost?

Most of our homes land between $425 and $650 per square foot, depending on site conditions, finishes and complexity. We give you a realistic range at the first site walk and a fixed, line-item price before construction begins.

Do I need to own land before we start?

No. We regularly help clients evaluate lots before they buy, covering slope, soil, septic, utilities, setbacks and views, so you know what a parcel can support before you commit.

How long does the whole process take?

Plan on three to four months for design, selections and permitting, then ten to fourteen months of construction. Your schedule is mapped week by week and shared in your client portal.

What happens if costs change during the build?

Your contract price is fixed. If you request a change, we price it in writing and you approve it before any work happens. Unforeseen site conditions are documented with photos and reviewed with you first.

Can I visit the site while my home is being built?

Absolutely. We schedule regular walkthroughs with your superintendent at key milestones, and you are welcome on site any time with a quick heads-up for safety.
