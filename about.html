About

A studio built
to launch work.

Brandon Fonville Creative Studio operates like a small professional design agency, not a one-off freelancer. Every claim of brand, product, or digital work is backed by discovery, proposal, contract, structured revisions, and production-ready handoff.

The work turns ideas into clean, functional, and visually impactful designs that help businesses and creators bring concepts to life in a way that feels modern, intentional, and professional.

Beyond the visuals: project coordination, contracts, revisions, production prep, and print setup, with complete solutions ready for execution.

Studio pillars

  • Brand systems: identity, strategy, guidelines, launch assets
  • Product & digital: UI/UX, SaaS, websites, apps, prototypes
  • Physical products: apparel, packaging, merch, mockups, print
  • Contracts, proposals, and clear scope on every project
  • Consultations and website / digital experience audits
  • Retainer options for ongoing creative partnership
Who we help

Founders and teams who need design that ships.

  • Startups launching a product, SaaS platform, or brand from zero.
  • Small businesses ready to look professional online and in market.
  • Product companies shipping physical or digital products with UI, packaging, or merch needs.
  • Creators & lifestyle brands building apparel and consumer brands that feel premium and cohesive.
Process

How projects move.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Client completes the discovery questionnaire.

  2. 02

    Strategy call

    Align on problem, solution, scope, and success.

  3. 03

    Proposal

    Clear scope, timeline, investment, and next steps.

  4. 04

    Contract + deposit

    Signed agreement and deposit before work starts.

  5. 05

    Design

    Concepts, structured revisions, milestone refinement.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Final files: formatted, consistent, and production ready.
