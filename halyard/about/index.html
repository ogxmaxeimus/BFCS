About

An exchange that shows its work.

Halyard is a crypto exchange and Web3 wallet built in Lisbon on one idea: the price on the screen is the price you get. Two hundred markets, your own keys, and proof of reserves every month.

From one order book to two hundred markets.

  1. 2019
    Three people and one order book

    Halyard opens in Lisbon with eleven pairs and a rule that has not changed since. The price on the screen is the price you get.

  2. 2020
    Proof of reserves, every month

    The first attestation is published. Assets held against customer balances, signed by an outside auditor, twelve times a year.

  3. 2021
    The wallet

    Self-custody inside the app. Your keys, our order book, and a recovery ceremony a first-timer can finish.

  4. 2023
    Licensed in the EU

    Registration as a virtual asset service provider. Compliance moves to Dublin, custody to Zurich.

  5. 2025
    The launchpad

    Vetted token sales with a public audit for every listing. Meridian's sale closes in forty minutes.

  6. 2026
    Two hundred markets

    2.1 million accounts in 140 countries, 200+ assets, and still one order book per pair.

Three things we don't trade.

01

The real price

One order book per pair, no internal spread, no payment for order flow. The fill is the number you saw.

02

Your keys

The wallet is self-custody by default. We can freeze an order book. We cannot move your coins.

03

Proof

Reserves attested monthly, fees published quarterly, audits linked from the security page. If we cannot show it, we do not claim it.
