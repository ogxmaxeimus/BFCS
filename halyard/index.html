10 · Questions

Before you trade.

Something else? Write to support@halyard.exchange. A person answers within one business day.

01 Is the price I see the price I get?

Yes. The quote includes the fee, and a market order fills against the live book. For a fixed price, place a limit order.

02 How fast do orders fill?

The matching engine handles an order in under a millisecond. Network settlement for withdrawals depends on the chain.

03 Do I hold my own keys?

In the Halyard wallet, yes: it is self-custody, and we cannot move your funds. Balances on the exchange are held in segregated custody wallets.

04 What happens if I lose my seed phrase?

Nobody can recover a self-custody wallet without the seed. Write it down, store it in two places, and test recovery with a small amount.

05 What does a trade cost?

Tier 1 is 0.10% maker and 0.20% taker. Volume tiers lower it to 0.02% / 0.06%. Deposits are free; withdrawals pay the network fee.

06 Are there hidden spreads?

No. The fee schedule is the only fee. The spread you see on the book is the market's, not ours.

07 Where are customer assets kept?

98% in cold storage across three jurisdictions, 2% in hot wallets for withdrawals, all covered by monthly proof of reserves.

08 Is there an insurance fund?

Yes, $120M funded from fees, covering losses from a custody failure. It does not cover market losses.

09 Which countries can open an account?

Residents of 140 countries. The sign-up flow checks your country first, before any documents.

10 How long does verification take?

Most accounts are verified in under 10 minutes. Manual reviews take up to one business day.
